
Premium Health Plan - Policy Summary

Acupuncture Coverage:
Acupuncture treatments may be covered when performed at an authorized facility and when the treatment meets the plan's coverage conditions.

Network Restrictions:
Coverage may depend on the member's network and regional restrictions. Members should verify eligibility and facility authorization before receiving treatment.
