Health Insurance Policy

Policy Name: Premium Health Plan

Coverage:
The policy covers hospitalization expenses, emergency treatment,
doctor consultations, diagnostic tests, and prescribed medicines.

Alternative Treatments:
The policy covers approved alternative treatments including
Ayurveda, Yoga, Naturopathy, Unani, Siddha, and Homeopathy,
subject to the policy terms and conditions.

Acupuncture:
Acupuncture treatment is covered only when it is provided at
an authorized medical facility and meets the conditions specified
in the policy.

Exclusions:
Cosmetic treatments and experimental treatments are not covered.

Policy Region:
Coverage is applicable across the United States, subject to
network and regional restrictions.
